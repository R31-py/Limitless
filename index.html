HOW TO PLAY

Climb as high as you can. Press anywhere, drag away from where you want to go and let go. You shoot the opposite way, like a slingshot. The longer the drag, the harder the shot. The dotted guide turns from green (weak) to red (full power).

You can only aim while you rest in a bubble, are stuck to goo, or are frozen. Fall off the bottom of the screen and the run is over.

YOU & THE WORLD

  • YouThe ball. Gravity pulls you down the whole time, so every shot is an arc. Pick your skin on the start screen.
  • BubbleFly into one to catch it. Rest safely inside and aim your next shot. The bubble pops when you launch, so there is no going back.
  • Side wallsSolid. You bounce off and keep about two thirds of your speed.
  • Sticky goo (blue)Touch it and you stick. From there you can aim again, just like inside a bubble.
  • Bouncer (green)A springy pad on the wall. It throws you back faster than you hit it. Skim along it at a shallow angle and it flings you straight up.
  • Booster (pink)Fly through it to be blasted at high speed in the direction of your last shot. The arrows show that direction. One use only.
  • Floating obstaclesDark circles and diamonds that start to appear as you climb. You bounce off them and lose speed. If one has a blue patch, you only stick when you hit that part.

PICKUPS

  • CoinWorth +50 score. Grab several in quick succession and the pickup sound climbs in pitch.
  • Freeze power-upA rare floating pickup. Collect it, then tap its icon while you are flying to stop dead in mid-air. Then aim and shoot again.
  • Trajectory power-upCollect it and tap its icon to arm it. Your next aim shows the whole predicted path, including bounces and where you will land. It is used up when you shoot.
  • Power-up slotsThe three circles at the bottom of the screen. Dimmed icons cannot be used right now (Freeze needs you to be in the air).

SCORE

Score is the height you have climbed plus 50 for every coin. The screen shows your score, your BEST, your HEIGHT and your COINS. The wall lines slowly change color the higher you get.
